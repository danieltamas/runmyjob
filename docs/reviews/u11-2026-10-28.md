## Answers
- P0 AlertCenter secrets: fixed. The planner's secrets are the Keychain PATs of every destination's runner targets, read when a notice is built.
- P0 ConfigFile lock: fixed. read-merge-write holds a flock on `config.json.lock`.
- P1 delivery: authorization is requested once at start; a denied authorization is the owner's choice, so the notice is not retried. A failed `add` is not retried (accepted: one local banner).
- P1 unreachable dedupe: fixed. The key is persisted in `notified.json` with the announced job ids.
- P1 persistence errors: accepted. A lost `notified.json` re-announces nothing older than 10 minutes (first-call freshness).
- P1 eviction at 5000 ids: no change. Eviction removes the oldest ids, which are older than the freshness window and would be silent anyway.
- P1 availability from probes: accepted, open. Availability is the tailnet and peer state at every poll; an ssh-only failure on an online peer is not noticed.
- P1 Update opens the release page: by design until U12 (signing, installer); the in-place updater arrives with the signed release.
- P2 toggles: fixed. A change re-reads the file and sets only its key.

## Re-review (final diff)
- P0 redaction before sanitising: fixed. Text is cleaned first, then redacted, then bounded.
- P0 one-time handoff consumed by the secret provider: fixed. The provider reads the Keychain only, and a notice reads its secrets once.
- P0 denied Keychain read leaves a PAT unredacted: accepted. A PAT that cannot be read is not known to RMJ; it is the same inventory the runner registration uses.
- P2 toggle merge: fixed. `NotificationSettings.set` merges one key under the lock.
